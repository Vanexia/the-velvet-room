# Remaining-route review

11 October 2026. Scope: **23 July through 26 October**, covering **94 dates and 269 existing instructions**. Earlier dates remain unchanged. The website remains the maintained edition; the Google Doc is an unsynchronised backup.

## Why this review was needed

The July 23 merchant step required Imagination 3, but the player was at rank 2 with More's 15-Archetype task still active. The original schedule counted on that reward earlier. Our expansion made the task conditional while retaining the later rank expectation. The rewritten day checks the task and actual virtue rank before spending time, then provides a separate afternoon alternative and its later bond dates.

## Review method

The current original Google Sheet and owned Google Doc were read again. Each remaining instruction has a private record identifying its evidence, status and any proposed correction. Month reviews were followed by independent checks of follower prerequisites, activity periods, recovery conflicts, collections, cooking and party recommendations. The root reviewer reconciled the proposals; source agreement alone did not make a claim reliable.

The follow-up checks rejected two proposed changes: a follower event placed on the day immediately after its prerequisite, and removal of a real collectible as an apparent duplicate. Both demonstrate why the final route needs review beyond separate month-by-month checks.

## Changes

- Check earned rewards and current Royal Virtues before dependent events. Preserve the distinction between studying an Archetype, training it and reporting a completed task.
- Correct an informant's building and an ordinary conversation's virtue reward. Name quest recipients, districts and buildings where the old instructions were vague.
- Finish a book before the later event that needs its reward, while retaining all seven books' three initial reading sessions.
- Carry delayed follower events through their later town and travel requirements. Count arena matches actually completed, including matches used as alternative activities.
- Match party preparation to the selected day's possible outing and the arena rank shown in the game.
- Preserve five separate initial island beetles. Cooking recovery checks actual quantities, prior cooking quests, random bait and available travel periods.
