# The Velvet Room
Metaphor: ReFantazio | Personal platinum schedule | First-playthrough edition

## Start here
This is the complete, unhidden source for The Velvet Room's daily guide. It contains future gameplay details. Use the website to read one date at a time with spoiler controls, or stop this document at your current in-game date.

This personal revision of Goonan's Minimalist Schedule is adapted for Normal difficulty. Following the 7 October review, the remaining route from 23 July onward was reviewed again on 11 October 2026 against the current original Sheet, personal Google Doc and independent sources. Earlier dates remain unchanged. This route has not been played through end to end. Check your actual ranks and rewards before dependent events; follow the dated alternatives when a requirement is still missing.

Keep a manual save at the start of a free day, before a long trip and before completing a story deadline. Retain a few older saves. If you have already passed an instruction, first check your inventory and quest log; do not assume you must restart.

Dungeon days usually assume you complete the objective in one visit. Normal is suitable, but MP management and some extra fighting may be necessary. Optional shopping budgets, equipment and grinding are not requirements just because the original author recommended them. Keep some MAG for studying Archetypes instead of exchanging it all for money.

A second playthrough is required for the platinum. Save clear data when the game offers it. The details are at the bottom under “After your first completion”; leave that section until then.

## How to follow a day
Free errands: Walking around town, ordinary shopping, accepting requests and the named free hand-ins fit before the main activity. A conversation can change into a time-consuming event, so read the game's confirmation before accepting.

Afternoon / night: These are separate activity periods. Reading, cooking at the inn, a bond event or a virtue activity normally uses the current period. Do not choose an extra activity simply to make the clock match the guide. “Night: used by the dungeon” means you do not have another town activity after returning.

Ordered steps: Finish the listed errands before the next “uses time” activity or departure. “Optional” means you can skip it without removing a planned platinum step. A “checkpoint” asks you to inspect the actual result before continuing.

Report / hand in: Speak to the person named in the hand-in step or the quest log's current objective; this can differ from the original requester. Bounties go to a Recruiter Dispatcher: inside Grand Trad's Recruitment Centre, or outdoors in Martira's Thoroughfare Square. Collecting an item or defeating a target is not always the final step. Check that the quest is marked complete after claiming the reward.

Bond / target rank: This means a follower's rank-up event, not their character level or Archetype rank. Once available, use the Followers menu to locate the named person and travel to them. Look for the rank-up handshake icon and check any unmet requirement. Finish the event at the stated target rank; you are not being asked to gain that many ranks at once.

Dialogue choices: Pick freely during ordinary follower bond events unless a specific answer is given here. Their replies generally change MAG rewards rather than whether the bond ranks up. Answers written beside cooking, reading or debates serve a different purpose; follow those.

Study / train / master: Study spends MAG to unlock an Archetype for a particular character. Train means gain A-EXP in it or use A-EXP items. Master means reach Archetype rank 20. An Archetype becoming available through a follower does not mean you have already studied it. Check whose Archetype rank you are viewing.

Wisdom, Tolerance, Courage, Eloquence and Imagination are Royal Virtues. They are separate from the combat stats you raise when levelling up. Check their rank display when the schedule names a requirement; reward icons do not prove you reached it.

If something is unavailable: Check the date and time, previous bond rank, quest hand-in and required virtue first. Read the recovery instruction for that date. Do not spend the slot on an unrelated chat and assume the scheduled event happened. Protect dated events and request deadlines before optional shopping or grinding.

## After your first completion
Spoilers and return-run mechanics below. Read only after finishing the first playthrough.

Start New Game Plus from the clear data. Choose Easy for the simplest remaining trophy route, or Normal/Hard if you prefer. Do not choose Storyteller for this run: its exclusive challenge is unavailable on that setting. Regicide is an optional challenge setting selected when starting the run; do not rely on the source's claim that you can switch up to it later.

Advance far enough to access the Book of the Apocalypse in Akademeia and prepare for the Redscale Dragon. Its availability does not mean a low-level fresh return-run party can beat it. Rebuild the needed follower unlocks and train to a suitable level. PSNProfiles' dedicated section explains the fight; equipment that repels attacks can trigger a punitive response, so check your full loadout.

Finish the exclusive challenge and any remaining trophy. Books, requests and follower completion should be checked within a single run where required; do not assume two partial runs combine. Unspent beetles and several crafting/cooking records carry over, but check the specific trophy condition before planning a cleanup.
